About

Gozzy Nwogbo is a management consultant and technology entrepreneur based in Toronto, Canada. He holds an MSc in Digital Management from Ivey Business School and a BBA in Information Systems from Boston University. He applies McKinsey-style strategic frameworks to hands-on platform development across Nigeria's fintech, healthcare, and defense sectors, building with Python, React, Three.js, and LangChain. His current work spans a digital banking platform processing 10,000+ monthly transactions, a healthcare workflow system that achieved 35% efficiency gains across three pilot clinics, and a secure defense communications platform.

Background

My consulting training at Ivey taught me how to decompose complex business problems into structured analyses. Market sizing, competitive positioning, stakeholder mapping. The frameworks are rigorous and transferable.

My engineering work taught me something the frameworks do not capture: the difference between a strategy that looks right on paper and one that survives contact with real infrastructure, real users, and real network conditions in Lagos or Abuja.

I build at the intersection of these two tracks. The consulting side tells me which problems are worth solving and for whom. The engineering side lets me build the solution rather than hand off a requirements document and hope.

How I Work

I run a barbell strategy: conservative engineering rigor on one end, aggressive cross-domain thinking on the other. Every system I build starts with a spec that defines what "done" looks like before a line of code is written. The spec is the contract.

I think in connections. The queuing system I built for the fintech platform's NIBSS integration directly informed the offline-sync architecture for the defense platform. The healthcare workflow automation revealed patterns in how Nigerian organizations actually operate, not how Western software assumes they do. These cross-domain transfers are where the most interesting engineering problems live.
